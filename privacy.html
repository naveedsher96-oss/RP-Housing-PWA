Privacy Policy

Roshan Pakistan Housing Society App · Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Roshan Pakistan Housing Society app ("the App"), operated by the management of Roshan Pakistan Housing Society ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and password (managed securely by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Resident biodata: name, CNIC number, phone number, house/plot address, blood group, occupancy type, and family/dependent details, provided by you during registration.
  • Financial records: maintenance bills issued to you, fund contributions you submit, payment references, and payment proof images/documents you upload.
  • Activity data: complaints, poll votes, volunteer program participation, and notifications, all linked to your account.
  • Device token: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) if you enable notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity as a resident and approve your membership.
  • To issue and track maintenance bills and community fund contributions.
  • To handle complaints and community services.
  • To send you notices, bill alerts, and community announcements.
  • To maintain society financial and governance records (audit trail).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. Your data is visible only to:

  • Society management (admins and Board members) for administration purposes.
  • You, for your own records.

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) acting as our service provider, subject to Google's Firebase privacy terms.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by role-based Firestore security rules: residents can only access their own records, and only society management can view administrative data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial and governance records (bills, contributions, decisions, audit logs) are retained for the society's statutory record-keeping. You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting the society management at the email below; records required for financial audit may be retained in archived form as permitted by law.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult residents of the society and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced within the App.
